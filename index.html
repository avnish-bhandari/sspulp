About SSPU

Symbiosis Skills and Professional University (Formerly known as Symbiosis Skills and Open University) is a premier university that provides skill learning to students. It is the first Skill University that emphasizes on hands-on training to prepare young minds for the future. The 'State of Art' campus has been constructed on 15 acres of land with specialized laboratories, workshops, and centers of excellence having specialized skill training equipment and machines.

The University is offering Certificates, Diplomas, and Degree programs in high-growth sectors such as Automobile, Construction, Mechatronics, Computer Science & IT (Cyber Security), Architecture, Retail and Ecommerce, Ports & Terminal Management, Beauty & Wellness, Data Science, Digital Media, Nutritional Sciences etc. with multi entry-exit and credit banking options.
